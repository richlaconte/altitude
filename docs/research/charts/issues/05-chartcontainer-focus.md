## Component

`ChartContainer`, and the chart types that draw marks in a series colour: `BarChart`, `LineChart`, `AreaChart`, `ScatterChart`. They must change together because focus is resolved in the shared frame (colour assignment, legend, readout order) and drawn by each type. Includes one new token, `data-context`, which is a `Tokens:` change and can't ship separately because nothing else would use it.

## What you're trying to do

Say which series or records are the point of a chart. Altitude's first law is "altitude is importance", but a chart's marks can't rise, so today **nothing in a chart can matter more than anything else**: every series wears a saturated identity colour at full strength. That's the main reason the charts read as generic. The grouped, stacked, area and scatter previews look like any library's categorical defaults.

The common practitioner fix is one hue for the story and grey for the context (Knaflic, *Storytelling with Data*; the FT's and Datawrapper's house styles). In a controlled test, "focus" (a distinct colour on the subset that matters, with a headline and annotation) strongly shaped which pattern people remembered and raised clarity and aesthetics ratings ([Ajani et al., TVCG 2021](https://visualcognition.psych.northwestern.edu/publications/Ajani_Declutter_2021.pdf)). When the caption and the chart emphasise the same feature, that's the takeaway ([Kim, Setlur & Agrawala, CHI 2021](https://hci.stanford.edu/publications/paper.php?id=429)). Authors can't judge salience for readers ([Xiong et al., TVCG 2020](https://visualcognition.psych.northwestern.edu/publications/XiongCurse2020.pdf)), so the emphasis has to be declared, not hoped for.

The workaround today is to hand-pick `color` in the config, which breaks "colour follows the entity" silently and has no neutral token to pick.

## Today

- Colour comes from `ChartConfig` slots: `data-1`…`data-6`, then `data-other` (`chart.tsx:13–24`).
- Receding exists, but means something else: `relevance` and `selected` recede misses to 35% opacity of their own colour (E40, E41).
- `data-other` is not a context colour: it's near-black in the light schemes (L 0.33–0.35), because every lighter neutral that held 3:1 was within ΔE 15 of a slot (E23).

## Proposal

```tsx
// Series focus: EU is the story, the other regions are context.
<LineChart data={requests} x="day" focus={['eu']} />

// Record focus: on a one-series chart, the categories the takeaway names.
<BarChart data={deploys} x="team" focus={(d) => d.team === 'Cedar' || d.team === 'Delta'} />
```

- **`focus?: string[] | ((d: ChartDatum) => boolean)`** on each chart type. Series keys keep their slot colours; every other series is drawn in `data-context`. With a predicate, matching records keep their colour and the rest take `data-context`.
- **Labels follow focus.** Focused series keep their direct labels in `ink`; context series' end labels and legend keys drop to `ink-muted`. The readout lists focused series first.
- **Words, not only colour.** The generated summary starts "Highlighted: EU." The legend marks focused keys as "highlighted" in their accessible description.
- **Combines with filtering.** A context mark that misses a filter recedes to 35% of `data-context`, so "the author's context" (neutral, full strength) and "your filter" (receded) stay two readings.

**New token `data-context`**, generated by `build/palette.mjs` in every scheme, with these gates:
- neutral (chroma ≈ 0), so it's never read as a series;
- ≥ 3:1 against `surface-ground` and `surface-raised` (WCAG 1.4.11; Charts' "marks hold 3:1");
- visibly distinct from `line` (gridlines) and from `data-other`;
- **open:** E23 found no lighter neutral at 3:1 that is ΔE ≥ 15 from every slot. Propose that `data-context` is exempt from the slot-distance gate, because it's never the only cue for a series: context series are unlabelled or labelled in `ink-muted`. Record that as a measured decision in Research.md, as E23's loosening to 12 was.

**Rules it strains.** **Charts rule 5, "Colour follows the entity: hiding or filtering a series never repaints the others."** Focus does repaint the series that aren't focused. The argument for the exception: the rule protects a reader's filter from changing what they've learned, and focus isn't a filter. It's the author's statement of importance, fixed for the chart rather than toggled by the reader, and every repaint it causes cross-fades. Charts.md would need to say this explicitly: focus is identity for the few and one shared colour for the rest, and it isn't offered as a reader control (a reader's version is `relevance`).

**Additive?** Yes. Without `focus`, colours are unchanged.

## Elevation impact

None to tiers or levels: every part stays flat on the ground. It adds a colour state.

| Part / state | Tier | Level | Surface · shadow | Non-height cue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Focused mark | ground | 0 | flat slot colour · none | direct label in `ink`; "Highlighted" in summary and legend description |
| Context mark | ground | 0 | flat `data-context` · none | label (if any) in `ink-muted`; listed after focused series in the readout |
| Context mark, filtered out | ground | 0 | `data-context` at 35% · none | the frame's "4 of 12 match" count, as now |
| Legend key, focused / context | ground (static) or sunk + raised (toggle) | as today | as today; the swatch takes the mark's colour | accessible description "highlighted" |

## Motion and accessibility

- **Motion:** changing `focus` cross-fades fills and strokes over `dur-travel-1`, in place, as a filter does. Nothing travels, so it spends none of the attention budget. Under reduced motion it changes at once.
- **Keyboard:** unchanged path. Focused series come first in the series level of the keyboard path (summary → series → points).
- **Forced colours:** focused marks `CanvasText` with their patterns; context marks `GrayText`. Check that this stays distinct from the relevance fallback, which also uses `GrayText` (`bundle.css:1521`); if not, give context marks a sparse hatch.
- **`contrast="more"`:** no change beyond the token gates.

## Alternatives considered (optional)

- **Recede the context by opacity.** That's already the meaning of 35% (filtered or not selected). Reusing it would make "the author's context" and "your filter" one reading.
- **Use `data-other` for context.** It's near-black in the light schemes, which emphasises rather than recedes.
- **Use `ink-faint`.** Reserved for disabled labels.
